What Maxisave is

Maxisave is a Chrome extension, made by Scalere Design, that autosaves an in-progress Maxient incident report form so a refresh, crash, or closed tab doesn't lose what you've written. Signed in with Google, it keeps drafts, attachments, and submission history in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive so they follow you between computers. An optional "Format with AI" feature can send the incident description and involved parties' names/ID numbers to an AI service you choose, to rewrite it as a formatted report. See the privacy policy for exactly what data goes where.
