No servers · No accounts · No network requests

Maxient's reporting form has no draft feature

A refresh, a timed-out session, or an accidental back-button takes a half-written incident report with it — including every Involved Parties row you'd added. Maxisave fixes that quietly in the background.

What it does

Saves as you type

About 0.7 seconds after you stop typing, every 20 seconds as a backup, and once more when the tab closes.

Restores everything

Text fields, dropdowns, and every extra Involved Parties row you'd added — names, ID numbers, roles included.

Status you can see

A small pill in the corner: "Saving…", "Saved 3:42 PM", or "Draft restored from 3:12 PM".

Clear when you want to

One button wipes the draft and empties the form — including resetting the party rows back to one.

Gone on submit

The draft deletes itself the moment the report goes through.

Attachments are opt-in

Files aren't saved by default. Choose to keep them per draft, and you're warned before closing a tab with unsaved attachments.

Your data stays on your computer

There are no servers and no accounts. Maxisave makes no network requests at all — everything lives in your own browser profile, so a draft saved on one computer can't be opened on another. Session security tokens and the reCAPTCHA response are deliberately never saved. Full details on the privacy page.

Install

  1. Get it from the Chrome Web Store (link goes live once the listing is published).
  2. Open a Maxient incident report form — that's it, Maxisave is already watching.
